# Product brief

## Working name
CommerceOps Bridge — the agent-native merchant control plane.

## Problem
As an ecommerce store grows, merchants accumulate orders, payment events, policies, apps, fees, and support cases. Resolving exceptions requires manual context switching and can lead to slow or inconsistent decisions.

## Target user
Small and mid-sized ecommerce merchants, support leads, and operations specialists.

## Product promise
An agent gathers relevant evidence, explains the issue, proposes a safe next action, and prepares the work for human approval through structured WebMCP tools.

## Primary workflows
1. Payment/refund exception resolution.
2. App and operating-cost audit.
3. Support-escalation preparation.

## Non-goals
- Executing real refunds, payments, or account changes.
- Sending real customer or support messages.
- Storing real customer data.
- Replacing Shopify or any commerce platform.
